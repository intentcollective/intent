How We Work

Principal Design Fellows lead the pillars and host the debates, and Design Fellows contribute across them. Below, three disciplines describe what we study, and three methods describe how we learn.

Long before tuition and campuses, people learned by gathering and debating in the open. We work the same way, free and open to every designer.

Live Debate

A live debate is a structured, real-time exchange of ideas among designers around a defined question.

  • Principal Design Fellows select topics and lead debates within their pillars
  • One hour, online
  • Up to 100 participants
  • Times are shown in US Eastern, London, and Seoul. The Join link opens one hour before
  • Every participant is free to speak. Plain language, no lectures
  • The host closes on what to take back to your work

Roles

Anyone can take part in the debates. Fellowship is a selective appointment based on professional merit, intellectual contribution, and commitment to advancing design.

Participants

Get notices of upcoming debates. Join any debate that interests you.

You confirm by email first, and every notice has an unsubscribe link.

Design Fellows

Take part in as many debates as they can. Propose subjects and ideas to the Principal Design Fellows.

Principal Design Fellows

Lead a pillar. Propose its debate subjects and host debates.

Fellows receive an email notice of every upcoming debate.

Intellectual Foundation

Three disciplines describe what we study together.

Strategy

Intellectually sophisticated and critically reasoned.

  • Sociocultural intent
  • Critical inquiry
  • Human strategy

Literacy

Structurally informed and communicatively fluent.

  • Linguistic typography
  • Aesthetic taste
  • Systemic scalability

Synthesis

Critically discerning and integratively resolved.

  • Design critique
  • Design judgment
  • Design communication

Methodology

We learn by questioning, by debating, and by practice.

Discourse

Questioning and exchanging ideas.

Intellectually open and collectively generative.

Debate

Testing and examining perspectives.

Critically challenging and constructively progressive.

Praxis

Putting ideas into practice.

Aesthetically authentic and experientially impactful.
