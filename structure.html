How the Collective Works

INTENT COLLECTIVE™ brings designers together through open public participation and a selective Fellowship. Anyone can take part in its live debates. Fellows lead them.

Its primary activity is live critical debate. Its intellectual foundation is Strategy, Literacy, and Synthesis, the three disciplines through which it examines design. Its methodology is Discourse, Debate, and Praxis: think, challenge, and make. Principal Design Fellows lead the pillars of inquiry and host the debates, and Design Fellows contribute across them.

Live Critical Debate

A live critical debate is a structured, real-time exchange of ideas among designers around a defined question, subject, or proposition. It is free, online, and open to everyone.

What it encourages

  • Critical examination of ideas
  • Different professional and cultural perspectives
  • Constructive disagreement and intellectual tension
  • Reasoned arguments and thoughtful questioning
  • Exchange of professional experience
  • Collective learning and the advancement of design thinking

How it works

  • Each debate has a subject and a Discourse post that frames the question.
  • Principal Design Fellows set the topics within their pillars, and host and facilitate the debates.
  • Each Principal Design Fellow hosts a one-hour online debate about every three to four months, usually on a Saturday morning, US Eastern Time.
  • Times are shown for US Eastern, Seoul, and London.
  • Anyone can join with the debate's Join online link. No Fellowship or invitation is needed. How you take part, speaking or listening, depends on the debate.
  • The conversation can continue in Discourse.

Fellowship

Leadership carries responsibility. Fellowship carries participation. Neither limits intellectual contribution.

Fellowship is a selective appointment based on professional merit, intellectual contribution, and commitment to advancing design. It is not required to take part in debates or any public activity.

Principal Design Fellows ⊙

Intellectual and organizational leadership.

  • Establish and develop the collective's intellectual direction
  • Lead the collective's pillars and areas of inquiry
  • Initiate and host critical debates
  • Support the development of Design Fellows
  • Maintain the integrity and continuity of the collective

Design Fellows ⌗

Professional knowledge, experience, and intellectual perspectives.

  • Take part in critical debates and discourse
  • Contribute to the collective's intellectual development
  • Propose debate subjects and areas of inquiry
  • Share professional experience and perspectives
  • Support collective activities and development

For now, Principal Design Fellows lead the debates. As the fellowship grows, Design Fellows will be able to reach out to pillar leaders to propose a debate.

Intellectual Foundation

Three disciplines give the collective a shared foundation for examining and advancing design.

Strategy ◉

Intentionality, focus, direction, and purpose.

Intellectually sophisticated and critically reasoned.

  • Sociocultural intent
  • Critical inquiry
  • Human strategy

Literacy ≡

Structure, organization, comprehension, and legibility.

Structurally informed and communicatively fluent.

  • Linguistic typography
  • Aesthetic taste
  • Systemic scalability

Synthesis ⊕

Integration, convergence, and resolution of ideas.

Critically discerning and integratively resolved.

  • Design critique
  • Design judgment
  • Design communication
Methodology

Discourse creates the conditions for exploration and exchange. Debate challenges ideas through critical examination. Praxis brings ideas into action through making and application. Together they form an ongoing process of questioning, examining, and advancing design. They are methods, separate from the pillars and from the Fellowship roles.

Discourse ∴

Deep thinking, reasoning, reflection, and intellectual exploration.

Learning through questioning and exchanging ideas. Intellectually open and collectively generative.

Debate ⇄

Critical examination, opposition, tension, and challenge.

Learning through challenging and examining perspectives. Critically challenging and constructively progressive.

Praxis ⌘

Making, action, experimentation, and application.

Learning through making and doing. Aesthetically authentic and experientially impactful.

Becoming a Design Fellow

Fellows are appointed on four criteria: professional merit, intellectual contribution, commitment to advancing design, and willingness to contribute to the collective. Becoming a Design Fellow is not required to take part in public activities.

Interested in becoming a fellow? Explore our praxis and discourse, collaborate with us, and become part of the Intent Collective community. Please contact us at intentcollectivedesign@gmail.com.
