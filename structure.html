How We Work

We bring designers together through open live debate and a selective Fellowship. Principal Design Fellows lead the pillars and host the debates, and Design Fellows contribute across them. Below, three disciplines describe what we study, and three methods describe how we learn.

Long before tuition and campuses, people learned by gathering and debating in the open. We work the same way, free and open to every designer.

Live Debate

What it is

A live debate is a structured, real-time exchange of ideas among designers around a defined question, subject, or proposition. It is free, online, and open to everyone.

How it works

  • Principal Design Fellows select topics and lead debates within their pillars
  • Each hosts a one-hour online debate every three to four months
  • Up to 100 participants. The call ends at one hour
  • Times are shown in US Eastern, Seoul, and London. The Join link opens one hour before
  • Every participant is free to speak

Fellowship

Leadership carries responsibility. Fellowship carries participation. Neither limits intellectual contribution.

Fellowship is a selective appointment based on professional merit, intellectual contribution, and commitment to advancing design. It is not required to take part in debates or any public activity.

Principal Design Fellows

Distinguished voices shaping the collective’s pillars.

They lead a pillar, set debate topics, and host debates.

They keep the role by hosting, and recommend their successor when they step back.

Design Fellows

Designers advancing design practice together.

They take part across all pillars, questioning assumptions, sharing experience, and turning ideas into practice.

Fellows receive an email notice of every upcoming debate. For now, Principal Design Fellows lead the debates. As the fellowship grows, Design Fellows will be able to reach out to pillar leaders to propose one.

Become a Fellow

Fellows are appointed for their professional merit, intellectual contribution, commitment to advancing design, and willingness to contribute to the collective. Becoming a Design Fellow is not required to take part in public activities.

Interested in being a Fellow? Take part in the debates and the Discourse, then write to us at intentcollectivedesign@gmail.com.
