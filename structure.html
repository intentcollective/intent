How the Collective Works

INTENT COLLECTIVE™ brings designers together through open public participation and a selective Fellowship. Its primary activity is live debate. Strategy, Literacy, and Synthesis are the disciplines it examines design through, and Discourse, Debate, and Praxis are the methods it works by. Principal Design Fellows lead the pillars and host the debates, and Design Fellows contribute across them.

Live Debate

A live debate is a structured, real-time exchange of ideas among designers around a defined question, subject, or proposition. It is free, online, and open to everyone.

  • Principal Design Fellows select topics and lead debates within their pillars.
  • Each Principal Design Fellow hosts a one-hour online debate every three to four months.
  • Schedules show US Eastern, Seoul, and London times.
  • Participation depends on the debate format.

Fellowship

Leadership carries responsibility. Fellowship carries participation. Neither limits intellectual contribution.

Fellowship is a selective appointment based on professional merit, intellectual contribution, and commitment to advancing design. It is not required to take part in debates or any public activity.

Principal Design Fellows ◉

Distinguished voices shaping the collective's pillars.

They lead a pillar, set debate topics, and host debates.

Design Fellows ○

Designers advancing design practice together.

They take part across all pillars: questioning assumptions, sharing experience, and turning ideas into practice.

Fellows receive an email notice of every upcoming debate. For now, Principal Design Fellows lead the debates. As the fellowship grows, Design Fellows will be able to reach out to pillar leaders to propose one.

Becoming a Design Fellow

Fellows are appointed on four criteria: professional merit, intellectual contribution, commitment to advancing design, and willingness to contribute to the collective. Becoming a Design Fellow is not required to take part in public activities.

Interested in becoming a fellow? Explore our praxis and discourse, collaborate with us, and become part of the Intent Collective community. Please contact us at intentcollectivedesign@gmail.com.

Methodology

Discourse creates the conditions for exploration and exchange. Debate challenges ideas through critical examination. Praxis brings ideas into action. Together they form an ongoing process of questioning, examining, and advancing design.

Discourse ∴

Learning through questioning and exchanging ideas.

Intellectually open and collectively generative.

Debate ⇄

Learning through challenging and examining perspectives.

Critically challenging and constructively progressive.

Praxis ⌘

Learning through making and doing.

Aesthetically authentic and experientially impactful.
