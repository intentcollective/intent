Principal Design Fellow Guide

How to lead a pillar, host its debates, and keep the schedule right. Everything here happens on the site and in three shared sheets.

Your role

You lead a pillar, set its debate questions, and host its debates. The pillars and the Principal Design Fellows who lead them are listed on the Structure page. Pillars >

Required

  • Host a debate every two to four months.
  • Take part in as many other debates as you can.

Encouraged

  • Post to Discourse beyond your own debates, with ideas, questions, and work in progress.
  • Share design news on Zeitgeist.

The debate

One hour, online, on one question, for up to 100 participants. The call ends at 60 minutes for everyone, so keeping time is the host’s responsibility.

10 minutes

You introduce the question and the positions.

40 minutes

Participants debate. You moderate and keep time.

10 minutes

You close with where the debate landed.

Where to do things

All of these are on intentcollective.design. Each form asks for the posting password, which is in the Principal Design Fellow Guide in Google Drive. Keep it private and never post it anywhere.

Every debate starts with a Discourse post. The scheduling form asks for its link.

  1. Post the debate on Discourse. On the Discourse page, choose Share a post. Include the one-sentence question, the background, and who may speak. Posts cannot be edited on the site. To correct one, edit its row in the Discourse sheet. Discourse >
  2. Schedule the debate. On the Structure page, under Upcoming Live Debates, choose Schedule a debate. Enter the date and time in US Eastern, and link your Discourse post and your own meeting link. Check the date and time before you submit, because any later change is emailed to everyone. Schedule a debate >

Also on the site

  • Register a new Fellow. On the Structure page, under Become a Fellow, choose Register a Fellow. Enter the Fellowship level, name, email, LinkedIn, and recommending Fellow. Register a Fellow >
  • Post other ideas or share news. Use Share a post on Discourse, or Share a link on Zeitgeist for news. Encouraged, not required. Zeitgeist >

After you schedule

  • Email notices, a reminder 24 hours before, and the public calendar entry all happen automatically.
  • Your meeting link appears on the site one hour before the start. It is never emailed.

Running the debate

One hour, a hard stop, and no warm-up. Every minute goes to the question.

Before

  • Create the meeting in Google Meet with your personal Google account. It allows up to 100 participants and ends automatically at 60 minutes for everyone.
  • In Host controls, set Meeting access to Restricted. Nobody can join before you, and people wait in the waiting room until you open.
  • Enter the meeting link when you schedule, or add it later in the Debate Schedule sheet.

Expected

  1. Open the call at the exact start time, not before.
  2. Choose Admit all to let the waiting room in.
  3. Start right away with the people there. No icebreaker and no small talk. Go straight to the question.
  4. Keep to 10, 40, and 10 minutes. The call ends at one hour wherever the debate is, so keeping time is yours.

Recommended

  • Admit late arrivals as they knock, without stopping the debate.
  • Begin your closing on time, so it is heard in full before the hard stop.

Change or cancel a debate

Open the Debate Schedule sheet and find your debate’s row.

  • New date or time. Edit the Date cell, written as 2027-04-03 10:00 (US Eastern, 24-hour).
  • Cancel. In the Status cell, choose cancelled.
  • Undo a cancellation. Choose scheduled again.
  • Subject, host, Discourse link, or meeting link. Edit the cell. No email is sent for these.

Within an hour, everyone is emailed, reminders are updated, and the site and the public calendar follow.

Rules

  • Never delete a row. Nobody would be told. Cancel instead.
  • Change things only in the sheet, never in the calendar.
  • Leave the grey columns alone (Added, Event ID, Announced, Debate ID). The script keeps them.
