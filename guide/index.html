Principal Design Fellow Guide

What you do, and how to do it.

Your role

  • Lead a pillar and host a debate every three to four months. You keep the role by hosting. Pillars > · Schedule a debate >
  • Join other debates when you can. Posts on Discourse and news on Zeitgeist are always welcome. Discourse > · Zeitgeist >
  • When you step back, recommend your successor.

The debate

One hour, online, for up to 100 participants. Everyone is free to speak.

10 minutes

You introduce the statement.

40 minutes

Everybody debates. You moderate and keep time.

10 minutes

You close with where the debate landed.

Prepare

  1. Post on Discourse. Choose Share a post and include
    • One plain statement that designers at every level can argue for or against
    • A short background
    • Up to three questions
    Posts cannot be edited on the site, so make corrections in the sheet. Discourse > · Discourse sheet >
  2. Get a meeting link. Make it in Google Meet with your personal Google account, the default. Set Meeting access to Restricted, so people wait until you open. Zoom or another platform is fine if it runs a full hour with a waiting room. Zoom’s free plan stops at 40 minutes.
  3. Put the debate on the site. On the Structure page, choose Schedule a debate. Enter the date and time in US Eastern, your Discourse post, and your meeting link. Check the date first, because any change is emailed to everyone. Schedule a debate >

Each form asks for the posting password, which is in the Principal Design Fellow Guide in Google Drive. Never share it. Notices, a reminder 24 hours before, and the calendar entry go out automatically. Your meeting link appears on the site one hour before the start and is never emailed.

Run it

  • Open the call at the start time and admit everyone at once. Start right away, with no small talk.
  • In the forty minutes of debate, speak least. Ask, and let others carry the debate.
  • Your questions are a plan, not a quota. If one carries the hour, stay with it.
  • Begin your closing on time. The call ends at one hour.
  • If the subject needs more, announce a follow-up debate and set it up the same way.

Change or cancel

Edit your row in the Debate Schedule sheet. Debate Schedule sheet >

  • New date or time. Edit Date, written as 2027-04-03 10:00 (US Eastern, 24-hour).
  • Cancel. Set Status to cancelled. Set it back to scheduled to undo.
  • Anything else. Edit the cell. No email is sent.

Everyone is told within an hour. Never delete a row, never edit the calendar, and leave the grey columns alone.
