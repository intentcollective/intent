Manifesto
Design everything.

INTENT COLLECTIVE™ is a design group founded on deliberate discourse in all things. Every expression, structure, and experience begins with defined intent. Its approach to design is grounded in intellectual sophistication and critical inquiry. Purpose precedes expression.

Designers and educators operate in alliance, advancing collective intelligence and design practice as forces for socio-cultural legibility, recognizing the altruistic value embedded in every communication experience.

rocess is systematic. Methodology is disciplined. Strategy informs structure. Structure shapes experience. Engagement extends to corporate institutions and cultural organizations through clarity and accountability, operating beyond conventional studio models. Independent investigations and self-initiated projects remain integral to the practice.

Three interconnected disciplines define the practice. Strategy establishes intent and meaning. Literacy develops the structural and communicative fluency through which intent becomes legible. Synthesis integrates critical inquiry, discernment, and resolution to bring meaning and expression into coherence. Together, they establish the intellectual and professional foundation of the collective.

INTENT COLLECTIVE™ advances education as a school of design, where design is understood not only as a practice but also as a way of questioning, thinking, learning, and living. The fellowship focuses on human experience through these interconnected disciplines. Rigorous, erudite thinking and principled execution are cultivated through co-education and collective inspiration.

Three complementary methodologies underpin this approach. Discourse, learning through critical thinking and radical inquiry. Debate, learning through challenging perspectives. Praxis, learning through making and doing. Together, they cultivate collective intelligence and continuous learning.

Design is intentional. Structured. Quintessential..
